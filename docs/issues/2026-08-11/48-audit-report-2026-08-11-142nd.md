## Source audit — surfaced F117 (client-side CSV formula injection)

Targeted pass over the carried-forward queue (R-141-3): all items re-verified at
source; **one was confirmed as a real emitted-artifact security bug and elevated
to a minted finding**:

- **F117 (security, P2): client-side `downloadCsv` bypasses the CSV
  formula-injection guard.** The server-side `escapeCsvField` (scripts/utils.js: 246) prefixes leading `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab with `'` (OWASP CSV Injection)
  and exempts negative numeric literals (F115). The client-side search-results
  export in `src/presenters/templates/homepage.js` (downloadCsv, lines 514–548)
  quoted every field but applied **no formula guard** — a school named `=SUM(1,2)`
  or `@evil` exported an executable formula cell evaluated by spreadsheet apps on
  open. Empirical proof: grep of `dist/index.html` showed raw `" + (s.a || '') + "`
  concatenation with no sanitizer; end-to-end vm simulation emitted
  `"=SUM(1,2)"` unguarded. **Fixed in this run** — mirrored the server contract
  client-side (`sanitizeCsvField`), applied to all 8 exported fields, added 4
  vm-isolated regression tests (scripts/homepage.test.js). Post-fix end-to-end
  emission: `=SUM(1,2)` → `'=SUM(1,2)`, `@evil` → `'@evil`, `-jalan` → `'-jalan`;
  full suite 1097/1093 pass; coverage 95.21/92.93; eslint 0/0; prettier clean.
  Merged via PR #680.
