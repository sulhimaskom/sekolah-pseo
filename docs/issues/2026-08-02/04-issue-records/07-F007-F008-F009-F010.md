## F007 — CI workflow overcomplexity
**Labels**: `refactor`, `P2` | **Files**: `.github/workflows/` (6 workflows, ~2045 lines)

- on-push.yml alone is 533 lines with 12 sequential `opencode run` flow steps, each up
  to 120min timeout. on-pull.yml runs the full ulw-loop on `schedule` (hourly) AND on
  every pull_request. Maintenance burden is high; blast radius of workflow edits is large.
- **Suggested fix**: consolidate the 12 flow steps into a single orchestrator script;
  reduce schedule frequency; extract shared setup into a composite action.
